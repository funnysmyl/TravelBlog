Top Reiseziele für 2026

What makes a beach truly breathtaking? It's rarely just the sand. It's the first glimpse of impossibly blue water after a long journey, the silence of an early morning before anyone else arrives, and the way the light changes the whole coastline at sunset. For this list I looked for places that have exactly that effect – beaches that stay with you long after the tan has faded. Some are world-famous icons, others still feel like a secret. All of them are worth the trip.

My top 5 beach destinations for 2026 at a glance:

  1. El Nido, Philippines – Hidden lagoons, towering limestone cliffs and crystal-clear water. The best way to explore Bacuit Bay is by kayak or on an island-hopping tour.
  2. Whitehaven Beach, Australia – Seven kilometres of pure silica sand in the Whitsundays, so fine and white that it stays pleasantly cool even under the midday sun.
  3. Elafonisi, Crete – Shallow turquoise water and shimmering pink sand, coloured by tiny crushed shells. Perfect for families, as you can wade far out into the lagoon.
  4. Anse Source d'Argent, Seychelles – Giant granite boulders, calm water protected by the reef and soft light that makes it one of the most photographed beaches in the world.
  5. The Maldives – Endless sandbanks, wooden jetties and house reefs right in front of your villa. Snorkelling here feels like swimming in an aquarium.
